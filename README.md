# Career Choice Website

Web using the Big Five Personality testing model in combination with RIASEC framework to recommend a career path of the highest suitability


### Structure
- javascript frontend + python backend

## Questions (30 total)
- 15 Questions for Big Five (3 per trait)
- 12 Questions for RIASEC (2 per type)
- 3 "Environmental" Questions (preference for remote work vs. on-site, corporate stability vs. startup risk, purely local vs. international environment)

### Big Five Questions (3 per type)

**Conscientiousness (Work ethic & organization)**

1. I see myself as someone who does a very thorough job.
2. I see myself as someone who is a reliable worker.
3. I see myself as someone who tends to be lazy. (Reverse)

**Openness to Experience (Curiosity & innovation)**

4. I see myself as someone who has an active imagination.
5. I see myself as someone who is original and comes up with new ideas.
6. I see myself as someone who has few artistic interests. (Reverse)

**Extraversion (Sociability & energy)**

7. I see myself as someone who is outgoing and sociable.
8. I see myself as someone who is full of energy.
9. I see myself as someone who is reserved. (Reverse)

**Agreeableness (Teamwork & empathy)**

10. I see myself as someone who is generally trusting.
11. I see myself as someone who is helpful and unselfish with others.
12. I see myself as someone who tends to find fault with others. (Reverse)

**Neuroticism (Stress tolerance - lower is generally more stable)**

13. I see myself as someone who gets nervous easily.
14. I see myself as someone who worries a lot.
15. I see myself as someone who is relaxed and handles stress well. (Reverse)

### The RIASEC Questions (2 per type)

**Investigative (The Thinkers - highly correlated with IT & Data)**

16. I would enjoy analyzing complex data to find hidden patterns.
17. I would enjoy developing a new scientific procedure or algorithm.

**Conventional (The Organizers - highly correlated with Finance & QA)**

18. I would enjoy keeping highly accurate, detailed records of business operations.
19. I would enjoy auditing financial systems to ensure absolute compliance.

**Realistic (The Doers - highly correlated with Hardware & Engineering)**

20. I would enjoy assembling complex hardware or electronic components.
21. I would enjoy operating and repairing heavy machinery or systems.

**Artistic (The Creators - highly correlated with UI/UX & Marketing)**

22. I would enjoy designing artwork, layouts, or branding for a magazine.
23. I would enjoy writing original content, scripts, or creative copy.

**Enterprising (The Persuaders - highly correlated with Sales & Management)**

24. I would enjoy starting, managing, and scaling my own business.
25. I would enjoy negotiating contracts and persuading clients to close a deal.

**Social (The Helpers - highly correlated with HR & Coaching)**

26. I would enjoy counseling people who are dealing with personal or career problems.
27. I would enjoy teaching, mentoring, or training others on new skills.

### The Environment/Context Questions (3 total)

28. **Remote vs. On-Site Preference**
Question: "I would much rather work entirely remotely from my own space than commute to collaborate in-person at a bustling office."

29. **Risk & Structure (Startup vs. Corporate)**
Question: "I prefer the fast-paced, unpredictable environment of a young startup over the structured, predictable stability of a large corporation."

30. **Market Scope (International vs. Local Czech)**
Question: "I want to work in an English-speaking, internationally focused environment rather than a company built specifically for the local Czech market."
